\section{Conclusion}

This study compares selected compact supervised forecasters and Chronos-T5-small with latest-close persistence for cross-market stock-price level prediction. Only 39 of 35,640 supervised configuration--seed rows achieve lower RMSE than matched persistence, and every supervised implementation has a median RMSE ratio above one. Chronos-T5-small passes in 119 of 990 sampled-origin rows, but its median ratio of 1.036 also remains above one. A small full-origin subset gives one pass in 60 rows and provides only a limited sensitivity check. For this target and these protocols, the results support baseline-first evaluation: claims of improvement should first establish lower error than persistence on the same forecast origins and target dates.
