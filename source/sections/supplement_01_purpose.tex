\section{Purpose and scope}

This supplement reports the corrected experiment and the scope of its verification. The main manuscript presents the central result, while the tables here retain list, horizon, feature, and seed distinctions. The experiment includes 35,640 supervised rows, 990 sampled-origin Chronos rows, and 60 full-origin subset rows. These rows form a fixed grid with shared assets and related configurations, not independent trials. The corrected local batch is \texttt{full\_corrected\_20261109}; the earlier public archive contains a different evaluation version.
